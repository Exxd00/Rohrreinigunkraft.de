# Nürnberg 30 km service-area release — 21 September 2026

The website now describes a 30 km straight-line radius from Nürnberg Hauptbahnhof (49.446389, 11.081944), following the existing operating geography. An included municipal centre does not imply that every district or address is covered. The exact property address is checked at the boundary. Distance is not driving distance or an arrival-time promise.

## Content and routes

- 97 place landing pages using the official Destatis municipality extract: territory date 31 December 2025, coordinate date 31 December 2024. Uninhabited municipality-free forest areas are excluded.
- 20 legally designated cities have eight core-service pages each, with 160 individually written intake briefs. Each city has its own access and planning guidance; shared technical procedures remain consistent.
- All 88 existing service/problem pages remain available. The eight main services have expanded explanations. This release does not multiply all 88 specialist topics across all municipalities.
- 355 public sitemap URLs: 10 general pages, 97 places, 88 service pages and 160 city-service pages. Modification dates refer to the release, not a daily freshness claim.
- New city-service pages replace broad redirects that previously blocked them. Specific legacy core-service URLs for the remaining 77 municipalities lead to their place hub. Outside-radius places are excluded; unknown paths return 404.

The directory has search, a city filter and a labelled schematic map. Pages keep the existing cyan/dark-slate design and add self-canonicals, meaningful titles, breadcrumbs, local navigation and structured service coverage. The inherited homepage canonical was removed. The footer map now points to the verified business address. Google showed 141 reviews on 21 September 2026; this is a dated snapshot.

Browser checks cover 1440, 768, 390 and 320 px widths, search, navigation, light/dark presentation and opening/closing the call dialog. No real form, call, email or conversion is generated by validation.
